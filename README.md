# ai-assisted-quarterly-business-review.
## Project overview
This portfolio project demonstrates how a Customer Success Manager can prepare a structured Quarterly Business Review (QBR) to review customer outcomes, product adoption, customer health, risks, and next-quarter priorities.

The workbook uses **Acme Logistics**, a fictional customer, and fictional sample data. It is a reusable template, not a live customer account or a deployed automation.

## What's included
- **How to use:** preparation checklist, AI drafting prompt, and data-validation reminder.
- **Account snapshot:** customer context and a 45-minute QBR agenda.
- **Goals and results:** progress against customer goals with status indicators.
- **Health metrics:** active users, time to first value, workflow completions, support tickets, NPS, and churn-risk notes, including quarter-over-quarter calculations.
- **Wins and feedback:** successes, challenges, customer feedback, and expansion/referral opportunities.
- **Action plan:** actions, owners, due dates, status, and next-quarter goals.

## Example workflow
1. Collect customer usage, onboarding, support, and survey data from source systems.
2. Populate the workbook and verify each figure against its source.
3. Use an AI assistant to draft a concise summary of wins, risks, and proposed next steps.
4. Review the AI output for accuracy, context, and customer confidentiality.
5. Run the QBR with the customer, agree action owners and deadlines, then send a follow-up summary within 24 hours.

## AI prompt example
> Using the customer goals, verified metrics, meeting notes, and feedback below, draft a concise QBR summary with the top three wins, top three risks, and three recommended next steps. Link each recommendation to a stated customer goal. Do not invent metrics; flag missing or inconsistent information for human review.

## Skills demonstrated
- Customer success reporting and QBR preparation
- Customer adoption and health monitoring
- KPI comparison and trend interpretation
- Risk identification and retention planning
- Action tracking and stakeholder follow-up
- Responsible AI use, including source verification and human review

## Automation opportunities (proposed, not implemented)
- Send reminders to action owners before due dates.
- Flag accounts with declining adoption, rising support volume, or overdue onboarding milestones.
- Create a draft QBR summary from approved source data.
- Create follow-up tasks after a QBR and track completion.

These are **future automation ideas**. The workbook itself is a template with spreadsheet calculations; it does not currently connect to customer systems or automatically send reminders.

## Data and privacy
All customer details and metrics in the workbook are fictional. Do not upload real customer data, personal information, or confidential business records to public AI tools or a public repository.
